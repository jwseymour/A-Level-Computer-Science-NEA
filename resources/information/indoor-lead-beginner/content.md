# Indoor Lead Basics

## Level Description
Beginning lead climbers are transitioning from top rope competency to leading. Focus areas include:
- Clip positioning and technique
- Lead-specific movement patterns
- Fall technique and safety
- Risk management

## Physical Benchmarks
- Comfortable top roping 5.10a
- Can climb 15+ moves without resting
- 3+ pull-ups for males, 1+ for females
- Solid core strength for stable clipping

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Lead-Specific Skills
   - Efficient clipping technique
   - Rope management
   - Rest positioning
   - Fall practice

2. Mental Preparation
   - Fear management
   - Focus under stress
   - Decision making
   - Risk assessment

3. Physical Development
   - Endurance building
   - Core strength
   - Grip endurance
   - Overall fitness

## Progression Strategies
- Gradual grade progression
- Regular fall practice
- Endurance training
- Mental preparation exercises
- Technical refinement
