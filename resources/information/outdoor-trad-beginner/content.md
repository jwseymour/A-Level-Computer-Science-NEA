# Traditional Climbing Basics

## Level Description
Beginning trad climbers focus on:
- Fundamental gear placement
- Basic anchor building
- Safety systems
- Conservative route selection

## Physical Benchmarks
- Leading sport 5.10c-5.11b
- Trad leading 5.8-5.9
- Ability to carry full rack
- Extended endurance for multi-pitch
- Comfortable with exposure

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Protection Skills
   - Gear placement
   - Anchor construction
   - Rope management
   - Gear removal

2. Technical Skills
   - Crack climbing technique
   - Route finding
   - Descent planning
   - Multi-pitch systems

3. Mental Preparation
   - Fear management
   - Decision making
   - Risk assessment
   - Communication skills

## Progression Strategies
- Guided learning
- Systematic grade progression
- Mental preparation
- Physical conditioning
- Technical practice
