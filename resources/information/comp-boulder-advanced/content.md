# Advanced Competition Boulder

## Level Description
Advanced competition boulderers excel in:
- Elite competition performance
- Advanced tactical decisions
- Time optimization
- Podium-level execution

## Physical Benchmarks
- Flash V7-V8
- Project V9-V11
- 12+ pull-ups
- Elite dynamic movement
- Competition-specific endurance

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Elite Competition Skills
   - Advanced tactics
   - Time optimization
   - Attempt management
   - Competition strategy

2. Physical Development
   - Power endurance
   - Maximum strength
   - Recovery capacity
   - Injury prevention

3. Mental Mastery
   - High-stakes performance
   - Pressure management
   - Strategic thinking
   - Podium mindset

## Progression Strategies
- National/International competitions
- Periodized training
- Mental coaching
- Recovery optimization
- Technical refinement
