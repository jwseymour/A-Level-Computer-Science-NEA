# Advanced Indoor Boulder

## Level Description
Advanced boulderers excel in:
- Complex movement sequences
- Power management
- Creative problem solving
- Technical mastery

## Physical Benchmarks
- Climbing V6-V8 consistently
- 10+ pull-ups
- One-arm dead hangs
- Complex dynamic movements
- Front lever holds

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Elite Movement
   - Complex coordination
   - Power precision
   - Micro beta optimization
   - Movement innovation

2. Physical Development
   - Maximum strength
   - Explosive power
   - Contact strength
   - Body tension mastery

3. Mental Advancement
   - Competition preparation
   - High-stakes performance
   - Project management
   - Flow state access

## Progression Strategies
- Specialized power training
- Competition participation
- Cross-discipline exposure
- Recovery optimization
- Technical mastery
