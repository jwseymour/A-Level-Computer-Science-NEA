# Intermediate Trad Climbing

## Level Description
Intermediate trad climbers demonstrate:
- Efficient gear placement
- Complex anchor systems
- Route finding ability
- Risk management

## Physical Benchmarks
- Leading trad 5.10a-5.11a
- Complex crack techniques
- Extended endurance
- Comfortable with varied protection
- Multi-pitch efficiency

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Advanced Protection
   - Specialized placements
   - Complex anchor systems
   - Gear selection efficiency
   - Natural protection use

2. Technical Skills
   - Advanced crack techniques
   - Multi-pitch efficiency
   - Route finding mastery
   - Rope systems

3. Mental Development
   - Head game management
   - Complex decision making
   - Partnership skills
   - Risk assessment

## Progression Strategies
- Regular leading practice
- Varied terrain exposure
- Mental training
- Physical conditioning
- Technical refinement
