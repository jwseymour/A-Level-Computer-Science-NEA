# Advanced Outdoor Sport

## Level Description
Advanced outdoor sport climbers excel in:
- High-grade redpoint climbing
- Complex sequence management
- Advanced safety systems
- Mentoring and route development

## Physical Benchmarks
- Leading routes 5.12c-5.13+
- 12+ pull-ups for males, 6+ for females
- One-arm lock-off ability
- Complex dynamic movements
- Extended endurance capacity

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Elite Techniques
   - Redpoint strategies
   - Complex beta sequences
   - Advanced rope management
   - Route development skills

2. Physical Training
   - Specialized strength
   - Power endurance
   - Recovery optimization
   - Injury prevention

3. Mental Mastery
   - High-stakes performance
   - Project management
   - Competition mindset
   - Teaching ability

## Progression Strategies
- Periodized training
- Project-specific preparation
- Recovery optimization
- Cross-discipline mastery
- Route development focus
