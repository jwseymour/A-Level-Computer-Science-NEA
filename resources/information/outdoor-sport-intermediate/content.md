# Intermediate Outdoor Sport

## Level Description
Intermediate outdoor sport climbers demonstrate:
- Efficient climbing on varied terrain
- Advanced anchor management
- Route assessment and planning
- Weather and environmental awareness

## Physical Benchmarks
- Leading routes 5.11a-5.12a
- 8+ pull-ups for males, 4+ for females
- 60+ second front plank
- Multi-pitch endurance
- Comfortable with long runouts

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Technical Skills
   - Advanced clipping techniques
   - Complex anchor systems
   - Route finding efficiency
   - Rock quality assessment

2. Physical Development
   - Power endurance
   - Core strength
   - Finger strength
   - Overall stamina

3. Mental Advancement
   - Fear management
   - Project strategy
   - Focus maintenance
   - Risk assessment

## Progression Strategies
- Systematic grade progression
- Regular endurance training
- Mental preparation exercises
- Technical skill refinement
- Cross-discipline exposure
