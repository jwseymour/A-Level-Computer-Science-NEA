# Competition Lead Basics

## Level Description
Beginning competition lead climbers focus on:
- Competition format understanding
- Pacing strategies
- Clipping efficiency
- Performance preparation

## Physical Benchmarks
- Onsight 5.12a-5.12c
- Redpoint 5.12d-5.13b
- 15+ minutes continuous climbing
- Complex sequence memorization
- Competition-specific endurance

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Competition Skills
   - Format understanding
   - Pacing strategy
   - Resting techniques
   - Observation skills

2. Technical Skills
   - Efficient clipping
   - Complex sequences
   - Volume technique
   - Quick reading ability

3. Mental Preparation
   - Competition anxiety
   - Focus maintenance
   - Strategic resting
   - Route memorization

## Progression Strategies
- Local competition participation
- Endurance training
- Mental preparation
- Technical refinement
- Recovery management
