# Competition Bouldering Basics

## Level Description
Beginning competition boulderers focus on:
- Competition format understanding
- Time management strategies
- Basic competition tactics
- Performance preparation

## Physical Benchmarks
- Flash V5-V6 consistently
- Project V7-V8
- 8+ pull-ups
- Dynamic movement mastery
- 4-minute max effort capacity

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Competition Skills
   - Format understanding
   - Time management
   - Attempt strategy
   - Zone/Top prioritization

2. Technical Skills
   - Competition-style movement
   - Volume technique
   - Coordination problems
   - Quick reading ability

3. Mental Preparation
   - Competition anxiety management
   - Focus maintenance
   - Recovery between attempts
   - Strategic planning

## Progression Strategies
- Local competition participation
- Mock competition practice
- Mental training
- Competition-specific training
- Recovery optimization
