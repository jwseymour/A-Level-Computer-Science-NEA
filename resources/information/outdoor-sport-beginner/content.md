# Outdoor Sport Basics

## Level Description
Beginning outdoor sport climbers focus on:
- Safety systems and gear management
- Outdoor-specific techniques
- Environmental awareness
- Risk assessment

## Physical Benchmarks
- Leading 5.9-5.10b indoors
- Comfortable climbing 20+ meters
- Endurance for multi-pitch routes
- Strength for carrying gear

## Key Areas for Improvement
1. Safety Skills
   - Anchor assessment
   - Gear inspection
   - Weather assessment
   - Emergency protocols

2. Technical Skills
   - Efficient clipping
   - Rope management
   - Route finding
   - Cleaning anchors

3. Mental Preparation
   - Height management
   - Exposure comfort
   - Risk assessment
   - Decision making

## Progression Strategies
- Guided learning
- Systematic grade progression
- Safety skill practice
- Mental preparation
- Physical conditioning
