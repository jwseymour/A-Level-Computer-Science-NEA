# Sport to Traditional Climbing Transition

## Program Overview
This 8-week program guides sport climbers through the transition to traditional climbing, emphasizing gear placement, anchor building, and risk assessment skills.

## Key Components
- Gear placement fundamentals
- Traditional anchor systems
- Route assessment and planning
- Protection strategy development
- Multi-pitch skills introduction

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive skill development
- Practical application
- Safety focus

## Prerequisites
- Solid sport climbing ability (5.11a/6c)
- Multi-pitch experience
- Strong safety foundation
- Mental readiness

## Safety Guidelines
- Always double-check gear
- Start well below limit
- Maintain redundancy
- Practice with experts

Remember: Traditional climbing requires comprehensive knowledge and experience. Take time to master each skill before progressing.
