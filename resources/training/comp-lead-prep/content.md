# Competition Lead Preparation Program

## Program Overview
This 6-week program transitions indoor lead climbers to competition-style climbing, focusing on the specific demands of competition environments.

## Key Components
- Competition-specific movement patterns
- Speed and efficiency training
- Mental preparation for competition
- Recovery and energy management
- Competition simulation training

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive intensity
- Competition-style sessions
- Recovery protocols

## Prerequisites
- Solid lead climbing ability (5.12a/7a+)
- Competition interest
- Time commitment
- Mental readiness

## Safety Guidelines
- Proper warm-up
- Fatigue management
- Technical precision
- Recovery priority

Remember: Competition climbing requires both physical and mental adaptation. Focus on gradual progression and consistent practice.
