# Indoor to Outdoor Bouldering Transition

## Program Overview
This 6-week program bridges the gap between indoor and outdoor bouldering, emphasizing safety, technique adaptation, and environmental responsibility.

## Key Components
- Outdoor-specific movement patterns
- Spotting and safety skills
- Environmental awareness and ethics
- Rock reading and assessment
- Project management

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive outdoor exposure
- Safety skill development
- Technical adaptation

## Prerequisites
- Intermediate indoor bouldering ability (V4/6B)
- Basic spotting knowledge
- Proper outdoor equipment
- Environmental commitment

## Safety Guidelines
- Always climb with spotters
- Proper pad placement
- Weather assessment
- Environmental respect

Remember: Outdoor bouldering requires patience, respect for the environment, and a strong foundation in safety practices.
