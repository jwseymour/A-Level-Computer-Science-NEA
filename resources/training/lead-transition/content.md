# Top Rope to Lead Transition Program

## Program Overview
This 4-week program guides climbers through the transition from top rope to lead climbing, with a strong emphasis on safety, technique, and mental preparation.

## Key Components
- Lead safety fundamentals
- Clipping technique development
- Mental preparation and fear management
- Structured fall practice
- Route strategy and planning

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive skill development
- Structured fall practice
- Technical drills

## Prerequisites
- Solid top rope climbing ability (5.10/6a)
- Basic climbing technique
- Physical and mental readiness
- Commitment to safety

## Safety Guidelines
- Always check equipment
- Maintain clear communication
- Follow proper progression
- Practice with experienced partners

Remember: Lead climbing requires patience, dedication to safety, and systematic skill development. Never rush the progression.
