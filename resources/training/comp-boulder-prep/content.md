# Competition Boulder Preparation Program

## Program Overview
This 6-week program transitions advanced indoor boulderers to competition bouldering, focusing on competition-specific skills and strategies.

## Key Components
- Competition-specific movement patterns
- Flash and onsight techniques
- Power endurance for multiple attempts
- Mental preparation and tactics
- Competition format training

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive intensity
- Competition simulations
- Recovery focus

## Prerequisites
- Advanced indoor bouldering ability (V7/7A+)
- Competition interest
- Time commitment
- Mental readiness

## Safety Guidelines
- Proper warm-up protocols
- Strategic rest periods
- Injury prevention focus
- Recovery management

Remember: Competition bouldering requires both physical and mental adaptation. Focus on quality over quantity and maintain strategic energy management.
