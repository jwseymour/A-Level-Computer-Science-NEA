# Sport to Multi-pitch Transition

## Program Overview
This 6-week program guides sport climbers through the transition to multi-pitch climbing, emphasizing rope management, anchor building, and route logistics.

## Key Components
- Multi-pitch rope management
- Anchor building and belay stations
- Route planning and logistics
- Partner communication
- Basic self-rescue skills

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive skill development
- Practical application
- Safety focus

## Prerequisites
- Solid sport climbing ability (5.11a/6c)
- Strong anchor building skills
- Basic rescue knowledge
- Mental readiness

## Safety Guidelines
- Always maintain redundancy
- Practice clear communication
- Check weather conditions
- Carry appropriate gear

Remember: Multi-pitch climbing requires thorough preparation and excellent communication. Master each skill component before attempting long routes.
