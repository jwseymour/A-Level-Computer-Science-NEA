# Indoor to Outdoor Sport Transition

## Program Overview
This 6-week program guides indoor lead climbers through the transition to outdoor sport climbing, emphasizing safety, route assessment, and environmental awareness.

## Key Components
- Outdoor safety systems
- Route assessment and planning
- Anchor management
- Environmental awareness
- Redpoint tactics

## Weekly Structure
- 3 training sessions per week
- Progressive skill development
- Practical application
- Safety focus

## Prerequisites
- Solid indoor lead ability (5.11b/6c+)
- Basic anchor knowledge
- Strong safety foundation
- Mental readiness

## Safety Guidelines
- Always check fixed protection
- Maintain clear communication
- Respect the environment
- Practice proper techniques

Remember: Outdoor sport climbing requires thorough preparation and respect for the environment. Master each safety component before pushing your limits.
