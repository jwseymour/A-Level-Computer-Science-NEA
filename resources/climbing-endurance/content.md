# 6-Week Sport Climbing Endurance Program

## Overview
This comprehensive program is designed to build climbing-specific endurance for sport routes, targeting climbers who want to improve their stamina on longer routes. The program combines various training methods to develop both aerobic and anaerobic endurance, while maintaining proper recovery protocols.

## Program Goals
- Increase forearm endurance and pump tolerance
- Improve recovery between clips and at rest positions
- Build aerobic capacity for longer routes
- Develop mental stamina for sustained climbing
- Perfect rest positions and breathing techniques
- Enhance movement efficiency
- Build antagonist muscle strength

## Weekly Structure
- 3 primary climbing sessions
- 2 supplementary training sessions
- 2 active recovery days
- Progressive intensity increase every 2 weeks

## Training Methods

### 1. ARC Training (Aerobic Restoration and Capillarity)
- Continuous climbing at 30-40% of max grade
- Focus on perfect technique and breathing
- Develop aerobic energy systems
- Improve capillary density in forearms
- Essential for recovery between hard burns

### 2. Interval Power Endurance
- High-intensity intervals on varied terrain
- Builds resistance to forearm pump
- Improves lactate tolerance
- Develops mental fortitude
- Progressive difficulty increases

### 3. Route Pyramids
- Structured volume at varying intensities
- Builds confidence across grades
- Improves route reading
- Develops pacing strategies
- Perfect for projecting preparation

### 4. Supplementary Training
- Antagonist exercises for injury prevention
- Core work for body tension
- Flexibility training for efficient movement
- Finger strength maintenance

## Recovery Protocols

- Proper nutrition timing
- Hydration strategies
- Sleep optimization
- Mobility work
- Self-massage techniques

## Progress Markers

- Number of routes per session

- Rest position efficiency
- Breathing control
- Technical precision under fatigue

- Adjust intensity as needed
- Take extra rest when required
